# Arrow tips with arrows.meta

Load `arrows.meta` and prefer modern tips such as `Stealth`. Encode relationship direction separately from color, dash pattern, or thickness; a theme change must not reverse or remove semantic arrows.

Keep arrowheads visually modest at journal size. Do not make every relation directed unless the user supplied directionality.

Source: PGF/TikZ 3.1.11a arrow/path material. Example: `arrow-meta`.
