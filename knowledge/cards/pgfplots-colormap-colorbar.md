# PGFPlots colormaps and colorbars

Use colormaps when color encodes a numeric value, and pair them with a readable colorbar or equivalent scale. Choose palettes that remain distinguishable at the intended output medium and do not add color when monochrome structure is sufficient.

For publication work, keep color limits and normalization explicit. A changed colormap must not silently change the mapped numeric range.

Prefer project themes or documented PGFPlots palettes over arbitrary decorative gradients.
