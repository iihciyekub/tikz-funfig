# PGFPlots fill between

Use the `fillbetween` library with named paths for confidence bands, uncertainty envelopes, or bounded regions. Keep upper and lower boundaries as explicit series so they remain inspectable and reproducible.

Use `soft clip` when the filled region must be restricted to an axis interval. Opacity is styling, not statistical meaning.

Do not label a filled region as a confidence interval unless that interpretation and level are supplied by the user or data.
