# PGFPlots group plots

Use the `groupplots` library for aligned scientific panels that should share dimensions, spacing, or axis conventions. Treat panel layout as structure rather than manually placing several independent axes.

Decide deliberately which labels, legends, ticks, or limits are shared. A compact multi-panel figure should still remain readable at the target single- or double-column width.

Do not merge panels that represent different scales or units without making those differences explicit.
